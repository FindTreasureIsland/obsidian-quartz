# Anthropic 产品团队为何比任何人都快

**How Anthropic's Product Team Moves Faster Than Anyone Else**

---

## Cat Wu 简介 | Introduction to Cat Wu

---

### 如何找到 AGI 的正确姿势

**Finding the Right Posture for AGI**

> **EN**: I think it is very hard to be the right amount of AGI pled. It's very easy to build the product for the super AGI strong model. The hard thing is figuring out for the current model, how do you elicit the maximum capability?

> **中文**: 我认为找到一个正确的 AGI 姿势是非常困难的。为超级强大的 AGI 模型构建产品是很容易的。真正困难的是：对于当前的模型，如何激发它的最大能力？

---

### Anthropic 的发布速度令人惊叹

**The Astonishing Shipping Pace at Anthropic**

> **EN**: I've never seen anything like the pace you folks at Anthropic are shipping at.

> **中文**: 我从未见过 Anthropic 这样快的发布速度。

---

### 消除一切发布障碍

**Removing Every Barrier to Shipping**

> **EN**: We want to remove every single barrier to shipping things. The timelines for a lot of our product features have gone down from 6 month to 1 month and sometimes to even one day.

> **中文**: 我们想要消除一切发布障碍。很多产品功能的时间线从 6 个月缩短到 1 个月，有时甚至缩短到 1 天。

---

### PM 角色正在快速改变

**The Rapidly Changing PM Role**

> **EN**: The PM role is changing a lot. It's changing really quickly. The thing that is extremely important for building AI native products is iterating so quickly, figuring out a way for you to actually launch features every single week.

> **中文**: PM 的角色正在发生很大的变化，变化得很快。构建 AI 原生产品最重要的事情是快速迭代，找到一种能真正每周发布功能的方式。

---

### PM 需要培养的新兴技能

**The Emerging Skills PMs Need to Develop**

> **EN**: It comes back to product taste. As code becomes much cheaper to write, the thing that becomes more valuable is deciding what to write.

> **中文**: 这归根结底是产品品味。当写代码变得越来越便宜时，决定"写什么"变得更加有价值。

## Anthropic 招聘 PM 的标准 | What Anthropic Looks for When Hiring PMs

---

### 技术变革速度加快的影响

**The Impact of Accelerated Technology Shifts**

> **EN**: I think before AI, technology shifts were a lot slower. So, you could plan on the 6 to 12 month time horizons. And because you were shipping features at a bit of a slower rate, there was a lot more emphasis on coordinating with all the other partner teams.

> **中文**: 我认为在 AI 出现之前，技术变革要慢得多。所以你可以做 6 到 12 个月的时间规划。而且因为功能发布速度较慢，你会花更多精力与其他合作团队协调。

---

### 时间线从 6 个月压缩到 1 个月甚至 1 天

**Timelines Compressed from 6 Months to 1 Month or Even 1 Day**

> **EN**: I think now with AI and with how much that has accelerated engineering and with how quickly the model capabilities are improving, the timelines for a lot of our product features have gone down from 6 months to one month and sometimes to one week or even one day.

> **中文**: 现在有了 AI，工程大大加速，模型能力也在快速提升，很多产品功能的时间线从 6 个月压缩到 1 个月，有时甚至到 1 周或 1 天。

---

### 快速交付比协调更重要

**Speed of Delivery Trumps Coordination**

> **EN**: And what that means is as a PM, there should be less emphasis on making sure that you're aligning your like multi-quarter road maps with your partner teams and more emphasis on okay, how can we figure out the fastest way to get something out the door?

> **中文**: 这意味着作为 PM，你应该少花精力去确保与合作伙伴团队的多季度路线图对齐，多花精力去想：我们如何能找到最快把东西交付出去的方法？

## 如何帮助团队快速前进 | How to Help Your Teams Move Fast

---

### 三个关键做法

**Three Key Practices**

#### 1. 设定清晰的目标用户和目标

**1. Set Clear User Goals**

> **EN**: I think a great PM is able to say, okay, our key user is professional developers. The main problem that we want to solve for this feature is maybe there's like too many permission prompts and people are feeling fatigue. And the use case is we want professional developers at enterprises to safely get to zero permission prompts.

> **中文**: 一个出色的 PM 能够说清楚：我们的核心用户是专业开发者。我们这个功能要解决的主要问题是权限提示太多，用户感到疲劳。使用场景是：我们希望企业里的专业开发者能安全地实现零权限提示。

---

#### 2. 用研究预览（Research Preview）快速发布

**2. Ship in Research Preview for Fast Iteration**

> **EN**: We clearly brand this when we ship something so that users know that this is an early product. This is just an idea. This is just something that we're trying to get feedback on and iterating on and that this might not be supported forever. And what this does is it reduces our commitment for shipping something. We can just get something out in a week or two.

> **中文**: 我们在发布时会明确标注这是研究预览，这样用户就知道这是早期产品。这只是一个想法，一个我们正在尝试获取反馈并迭代的东西，可能会不被长期支持。这样做降低了我们对某个功能承诺的负担，我们只需要一两周就能把东西推出去。

---

#### 3. 建立跨功能协作框架

**3. Build Cross-functional Collaboration Frameworks**

> **EN**: The third thing that a PM should do is help create the framework for the team so that they know when to pull in cross functional partners and what those cross functional partners expectations are.

> **中文**: PM 应该做的第三件事是帮助团队建立框架，让他们知道什么时候需要拉跨功能合作伙伴进来，以及这些合作伙伴的预期是什么。

---

> **EN**: So for example, we have a really tight process between engineering, marketing and docs. So when engineers have a feature that they feel is ready and that we've dog fooded internally, they post it in our evergreen launch room. And then Sarah who leads our docs and Alex who leads PMM and Tar and Lydia on DevRel just like jump in and can turn around the marketing announcement for it the very next day.

> **中文**: 比如，我们在工程、市场和文档之间有一个非常紧密的流程。当工程师觉得某个功能已经就绪，并且我们已经内部试用过了，他们就会把它发到我们的常青发布室。然后负责文档的 Sarah、负责产品市场推广的 Alex、以及 DevRel 团队的 Tar 和 Lydia 就会加入进来，第二天就能搞定市场公告。

## PRDs 和路线图在 Anthropic 的演变 | How PRDs and Roadmaps Have Evolved at Anthropic

---

### 两件事保持不变

**Two Things That Remain Constant**

> **EN**: So there's two things that we do. One is we have very rigorous metrics and we do metrics readouts with the entire team every week. The goal of this is to make sure that everyone deeply understands all the facets of our business.

> **中文**: 我们做两件事。第一，我们有非常严格的指标，每周与整个团队一起做指标汇报。这样做的目的是确保每个人深入理解我们业务的各个方面。

---

### 团队原则清单

**Team Principles Document**

> **EN**: The second thing that we do is we have this list of team principles. And this includes who our key users are, why those are our key users. And the reason that we articulate all of this is so that everybody on the team feels like they understand how our business works.

> **中文**: 第二件事是，我们有一份团队原则清单，包括我们的核心用户是谁、为什么他们是核心用户。我们这样表述的原因是，让团队中的每个人都觉得自己理解业务是如何运作的。

---

> **EN**: They understand what's important to us and what we're willing to trade off. And it lets people make decisions by themselves without feeling like they're blocked on PM or any other stakeholder.

> **中文**: 他们理解什么对我们重要，我们愿意做什么取舍。这样人们就能自己做决定，而不会觉得自己被 PM 或其他利益相关者卡住。

---

### PRD 仍然有用，但形式简化

**PRDs Still Have Value, But Simplified**

> **EN**: I think for features that are like particularly ambiguous, it does help to write out just a one-pager on what the goals are, what the delightful use cases are, what the failure modes currently are that we need to fix. And there are occasionally some projects, especially things that require heavy infrastructure that do take many months. And for those situations, we do write PRD still.

> **中文**: 对于特别模糊的功能，写一页纸文档说明目标是什么、令人愉悦的使用场景是什么、目前需要修复的失败模式是什么，这些确实有帮助。有些项目，特别是需要大量基础设施的项目，确实需要好几个月。对于这些情况，我们还是会写 PRD。

## Mythos 模型与 Anthropic 的发布速度 | The Mythos Model and Anthropic's Shipping Velocity

---

### 速度不完全来自 Mythos

**Speed Doesn't Come Solely from Mythos**

> **EN**: We've been moving pretty fast for several quarters now. So I think it's not fully mythos. Mythos is an incredibly powerful model. But we do use the models internally and I think this has increased our rate of shipping a little bit but I don't think it explains the bulk of the increase.

> **中文**: 我们已经连续几个季度保持了非常快的速度。所以我认为不能完全归功于 Mythos。Mythos 确实是一个极其强大的模型，我们确实在内部使用模型，这确实稍微提高了我们的发布速度，但我不认为这能解释大部分的提升。

---

### 真正的原因是流程和团队期望

**The Real Reason: Process and Team Expectations**

> **EN**: I think a lot of it is the process and the expectation on the team. So we're very low on process. We want to remove every single barrier to shipping things. We want to make sure every single person on the team feels empowered to take their idea from just an idea to out in the world in less than a week, sometimes even in a day.

> **中文**: 我认为很大一部分是流程和团队期望。我们的流程非常精简。我们想消除一切发布障碍。我们要确保团队中的每个人都感到有力量，能够在不到一周、有时甚至一天之内，把自己的一个想法变成现实。

**Four Major PM Teams**

> **中文**: 我们有几个 PM 团队，现在大概有 30 到 40 个 PM。

> **中文**: Diane 领导的研究 PM 团队，负责理解客户对我们模型的所有反馈，然后将其提供给最好的研究团队来采取行动，他们也负责主导模型的发布。

#### 2. 云开发者平台团队 (Cloud Developer Platform)

> **中文**: 云开发者平台团队维护着 Claude Code 底层的 API，也发布类似 managed agents 这样的产品——这是一种让你构建 agent、我们可以代为托管的方式。

#### 3. Claude Code 和 Cowork 核心产品团队

> **EN**: There's Claude Code that works on both Claude Code and the Cowork core products.

#### 4. 企业团队 (Enterprise)

> **中文**: 企业团队帮助所有企业客户更容易地采用 Claude Code 和 Cowork，包括成本控制、回滚机制、安全控制，确保这些企业对我们工具的使用感到非常自信和舒适。

#### 5. 增长团队 (Growth)

> **EN**: And then we also have our growth team that is responsible for growing across our entire product suite.

> **中文**: 还有我们的增长团队，负责在整个产品线中推动增长。

## 工程师与 PM 角色正在融合 | How Engineer and PM Roles Are Merging

---

### 所有角色都在融合

**All Roles Are Merging**

> **EN**: PMs are doing some engineering work, engineers are doing PM work, designers are PMing and also landing code. You can either hire a lot more engineers who have great product taste or you can keep your engineering hiring the same and hire a lot more PMs to help guide some of their work.

> **中文**: PM 在做一些工程工作，工程师在做 PM 的工作，设计师在 PM 工作并且也在落地代码。你可以雇用更多具有出色产品品味的工程师，或者保持工程招聘规模不变但雇用更多 PM 来帮助指导他们的工作。

---

### 招聘有产品品味的工程师

**Hiring Engineers with Product Taste**

> **EN**: On our team we're pretty focused on hiring engineers with great product taste. This way we can reduce the amount of overhead for shipping any product. Like there are many engineers on our team who are fully able to end to end go from seeing user feedback on Twitter through to like ship a product at the end of the week with almost no product involvement.

> **中文**: 在我们团队，我们非常专注于雇用有出色产品品味的工程师。这样我们可以减少发布任何产品的开销。我们团队有很多工程师完全可以端到端地完成工作——从看到 Twitter 上的用户反馈，到一周后发布产品，几乎不需要产品方面的介入。

---

> **EN**: I think like engineer and PM are kind of overlapping and you will get a lot of benefit from having more of either. I think product taste is still a very rare skill to have and we'll pretty much hire anyone who we feel has demonstrated this strongly.

> **中文**: 我认为工程师和 PM 在某种程度上是重叠的，多雇用任何一方都能带来很大好处。产品品味仍然是一种非常稀缺的技能，我们基本上会雇用任何我们认为强烈展示了这种能力的人。

---

## 为什么产品品味是最有价值的技能 | Why Product Taste Is the Most Valuable Skill

---

> **EN**: I still think it comes back to product taste. Like as code becomes much cheaper to write, the thing that becomes more valuable is deciding what to write. Like what is the right UX for this feature? What is the most delightful way that a user can experience it?

> **中文**: 我仍然认为归根结底是产品品味。当写代码变得越来越便宜时，决定"写什么"变得更加有价值。比如：这个功能的正确 UX 是什么？用户体验它的最愉快方式是什么？

---

> **EN**: We get tens of thousands of GitHub issues asking for every single thing under the sun and it takes a lot of care and taste to figure out okay which of these is worth building and what is the right way to build it and I think that that skill set can come from any background but I think that's the most important thing.

> **中文**: 我们收到了成千上万的 GitHub issues，涵盖了太阳底下的一切需求。弄清楚哪些值得构建、以什么正确方式构建，需要大量的用心和品味。这种技能组合可以来自任何背景，但我认为这是最重要的。

---

### 工程背景在当下的价值

**The Current Value of Engineering Background**

> **EN**: I think the reason why an engineering background is particularly useful at least for the next few months is if you have an engineering background, you have a better sense for how hard something should be. And that's often a factor in what you choose to build. So like if something is very easy to build, then maybe instead of debating it, you just spend an hour doing it.

> **中文**: 我认为工程背景特别有用的原因，至少在未来几个月内，是因为如果你有工程背景，你会对某件事应该有多难有更好的判断。这往往是决定构建什么的因素。比如如果某件事很容易构建，那与其争论不如花一个小时做掉它。

---

> **EN**: I think the valued skill sets does change quite frequently and so it's really hard to predict more than a few months out. It's less a commentary on what shift I think will happen and more of a commentary that I think large shifts will happen.

> **中文**: 我认为有价值的技能组合变化得相当频繁，所以真的很难预测超过几个月的未来。我不是在评论我认为会发生什么样的转变，而是在评论我认为会发生巨大的转变。

---

### 最重要的事情：基本原则思维

**The Most Important Thing: First Principles Thinking**

> **EN**: I think the most important thing is to be able to have this like first principles thinking where you can figure out how the tech landscape is changing what the team really needs from you and to like jump in and fix that hole because I think the work is becoming more amorphous.

> **中文**: 我认为最重要的事情是能够有基本原则思维，你可以弄清楚技术格局如何变化、团队真正需要你做什么，并投入进去填补那个空白，因为我认为工作正在变得越来越无形。

## 人类大脑将继续发挥作用的地方 | Where Human Brains Will Continue to Be Useful

---

> **EN**: I think humans still provide a level of common sense that the models don't. And there's like a thousand moving pieces to any product launch. Some of them are very small, but there's always a lot that could potentially go wrong.

> **中文**: 我认为人类仍然提供模型不具备的常识水平。任何产品发布都有无数个移动的零件，有些非常小，但总有很多可能出问题的地方。

---

> **EN**: I think the model doesn't always have a great sense of who all the stakeholders are, how they relate to each other, what their preferences are, what are the right venues to communicate with them to keep them on board.

> **中文**: 我认为模型并不总是能很好地了解所有的利益相关者是谁、他们之间的关系是什么、他们的偏好是什么、用什么正确的渠道与他们沟通以保持他们的支持。

## 在持续混乱中保持清醒 | How to Stay Sane in Constant Chaos

---

### 拥抱混乱

**Leaning into the Chaos**

> **EN**: I think our team is full of people who lean into the chaos. So, we try to face every challenge with a smile because there's always so much going on. If you get too stressed about anything you'll burn out.

> **中文**: 我认为我们团队满是拥抱混乱的人。所以我们尽量微笑着面对每一个挑战，因为总有那么多的事情在进行。如果你对任何事情都压力太大，你会倦怠的。

And sometimes they'll say that hey there was something confusing in the system prompt or I didn't realize that the front-end verification was part of this task or hey I delegated the verification to this sub agent and the sub agent didn't do the test and I didn't check its work.

> **中文**: 有时候当我注意到模型做了意想不到的事情时，让模型反思它为什么这样做实际上非常有用。有时它们会说："系统提示词里有些令人困惑的东西"或者"我没有意识到前端验证是这项任务的一部分"或者"我把验证委托给了这个子 agent，但子 agent 没有做测试，我没有检查它的工作"。

> **EN**: A lot of times just being very curious about why the model made the decision that it did will show you what misled it so that you can fix the harness in order to close this gap.

> **中文**: 很多时候，仅仅对模型为什么做出那个决定非常好奇，就能告诉你是什么误导了它，这样你就能修复 harness 来弥补这个差距。

> **中文**: 第三件有用但不是每个人都喜欢做的事情是构建 evals。你不需要构建数百个 evals 它们才有价值。只要构建 10 个出色的 evals 就能帮助团队量化目标是什么、他们在这方面的进展是什么、他们缺少什么。

> **EN**: I think eval is this underappreciated thing that more more PMs more engineers should be working on.

> **中文**: 我认为 eval 是一个被低估的东西，更多的 PM 和工程师应该做这件事。

## 新模型如何迫使产品改变 | How New Models Force Product Changes

---

### 删除不再需要的功能

**Removing Features That Are No Longer Needed**

> **EN**: A lot of the changes that we make with a new model is removing features that are no longer needed. So a lot of times we add features to the product as a crutch for the model because it's not naturally doing itself.

> **中文**: 新模型带来很多变化是删除不再需要的功能。很多时候我们给产品添加功能是作为模型的拐杖，因为模型本身没有自然做到。

---

#### To-Do 列表的例子

**The To-Do List Example**

> **EN**: The classic example for this is a to-do list. When we first launched Claude Code, people would ask it to do these large refactors and Claude Code would say, "Okay, cool. I need to change these 20 call sites" and it would go and change five of them and then stop.

> **中文**: 一个典型的例子是待办事项列表。当我们第一次发布 Claude Code 时，人们会让它做大型重构，Claude Code 会说"好的，我需要修改这 20 个调用点"，然后去修改了 5 个就停止了。

---

> **EN**: And then we were like, "How do we force it to remember to get every single one of these 20?" And so we added a to-do list and we found that with that Claude was actually able to fix all these 20 call sites.

> **中文**: 然后我们想："我们怎么强迫它记住要修改所有这 20 个？"所以我们添加了一个待办事项列表，发现有了这个功能后 Claude 实际上能够修复所有这 20 个调用点。

---

> **EN**: But then with Opus 4 and later models we realized that we didn't need to force it to use this to-do list. It would naturally use it itself. For the later models, without prompting, it just naturally thinks to do everything on the to-do list. These days, the to-do list is still nice to have as a user because then you can more clearly see what Claude is working on. But honestly, it's such a deemphasized part of the product right now that the model may use it, the model may not use it, it's not really necessary for it to make thorough changes anymore.

> **中文**: 但后来随着 Opus 4 和更新的模型，我们意识到我们不需要强迫它使用待办事项列表了，它会自然地自己使用它。对于更新的模型，不需要提示，它就自然地想到要做待办事项列表上的所有事情。现在，待办事项列表作为用户体验来说仍然是好的，因为你可以更清楚地看到 Claude 在做什么。但说实话，它现在是产品中一个非常不被强调的部分，模型可能用它，可能不用它，它对做彻底的修改来说已经不那么必要了。

---

### 新模型解锁全新功能

**New Models Unlock Entirely New Features**

> **EN**: The most exciting thing that new models unlock though is just like entirely new features. So there's a lot of features that we've been testing out with prior models and the accuracy wasn't high enough for us to want to launch them.

> **中文**: 新模型最令人兴奋的是解锁了全新的功能。我们用之前的模型测试了很多功能，但准确率不够高，我们不想发布它们。

---

#### Code Review 的例子

**The Code Review Example**

> **EN**: We tried to build a code review product a few times and we launched like simpler versions of code review in the past and it was only with the most recent models that we felt like okay this code review is so good that our engineering team relies on this code review to pass before we merge PRs.

> **中文**: 我们尝试构建代码审查产品好几次了，过去发布了更简单的版本。只有在最新的模型上，我们才觉得这个代码审查已经足够好了，我们的工程团队依赖这个代码审查来通过 PR 合并。

---

> **EN**: It was only with like Opus 4.5 and 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 that we felt like okay we are now able to run multiple code review agents simultaneously to traverse the entirety of the codebase and to synthesize a set of real issues that an engineer needs to address before merge.

> **中文**: 正是有了 Opus 4.5、4.6 和 Sonnet 4.6，我们才觉得我们能够同时运行多个代码审查 agent 来遍历整个代码库，并综合出一组工程师在合并前需要解决的实际问题。

---

> **EN**: It's pretty important to build products that don't necessarily work yet so that you know okay what is missing for this product to work and then with the newest model you can just swap it in to the prototype you've already made and see okay does this new model close that gap.

> **中文**: 构建不一定能工作的产品是很重要的，这样你就知道这个产品要工作还缺什么，然后用最新的模型把它换入你已经做好的原型中，看看这个新模型是否填补了那个差距。

## Claude Code 和 Cowork 的愿景 | The Vision for Claude Code and Cowork

---

### 阶梯式愿景

**A Step-by-Step Vision**

> **EN**: We think about this in terms of building blocks. For both Claude Code and Cowork the core building block is making individual tasks successful. So you want to produce some output, you give it a clear prompt description, is it able to consistently produce acceptable output that you're able to either merge or share with your colleagues or external audience.

> **中文**: 我们用构建块的方式来思考。对于 Claude Code 和 Cowork，核心构建块都是让单个任务成功。所以你想产生某个输出，你给它一个清晰的提示描述，它能持续产生你能合并或与同事或外部受众分享的可接受输出吗？

---

### 阶梯式演进路线

**The Step-by-Step Evolution Roadmap**

> **EN**: The task is the core building block. As the models get smarter, the task success rate gets a lot higher. And then we see people moving towards doing multiple tasks at the same time. So multi-coding was this big thing in towards the end of 2025 and it's only increased since then.

> **中文**: 任务是核心构建块。随着模型越来越聪明，任务成功率大大提高。然后我们看到人们转向同时做多个任务。多 coding 是 2025 年底的一个大趋势，而且此后只增不减。

---

> **EN**: And so we see this as okay great one task works and now you can do like six tasks at a time. As the models get even smarter the way that we are extrapolating this is okay next maybe you're going to run like 50 clouds at a time or hundreds of clouds at a time. And so what is the infrastructure we need to build to enable that?

> **中文**: 所以我们的看法是：好的，一个任务成功了，现在你可以同时做 6 个任务。随着模型变得更加智能，我们推断下一步你可能同时运行 50 个或数百个 Claude。那么我们需要构建什么样的基础设施来实现这一点？

---

> **EN**: At that point you're probably not going to run everything locally on your machine anymore. There's just not enough RAM to do it. And so we're thinking about how do we make it easier for you to manage all these? These will probably run remotely. How do we build the interface so that you as a human know which tasks you need to look into? How do we make sure that the agent is fully verifying work so that when you look at a task and it says it's done, you can very quickly verify and fully trust that it is done to your spec.

> **中文**: 到那时，你可能不再在本地机器上运行所有东西了，RAM 不够用。所以我们在思考如何让你更容易地管理所有这些？这些可能会远程运行。我们如何构建界面让你作为人类知道哪些任务需要关注？我们如何确保 agent 完全验证工作，这样当你看一个任务说它完成时，你能很快验证并完全信任它是按你的规格完成的？

> **中文**: 有时我看到用户尝试自动化某件事，达到了 90% 到 95% 的准确率就放弃了。如果自动化不是 100% 的时间都能工作，它就不是真正的自动化。最后那 5% 到 10% 确实需要更多时间。

---

> **EN**: I would encourage listeners to put in that time to scope some automation that you really want to get to 100%. Put in the elbow grease to teach Claude your preferences to give it feedback so that it can improve its skill so that it can get to that 100%. And then really then you'll be able to rely on it. There's just not much value in a 95% automation.

> **中文**: 我鼓励听众投入那个时间来确定你真正想达到 100% 的自动化。花功夫教会 Claude 你的偏好，给它反馈，这样它能提高它的技能达到那 100%。然后你就能真正依赖它。95% 的自动化没有太大价值。

> **中文**: 我看到很多人玩 AI 构建原型应用、修补工作流。我真的希望大家去构建你每天实际使用的应用，因为我认为只有通过那种使用你才能真正获得价值。

> **EN**: If you build a prototype app that isn't helping you get more done, then the AI isn't really adding value to your day. And there's only so much you learn from that when it's like, okay, I just one-shotted something. Oh, that's cool. And then you never come back to it.

> **中文**: 如果你构建了一个原型应用但它没有帮助你完成更多工作，那 AI 实际上没有给你的日子增加价值。而且当你只是"我 one-shot 了某件事，挺酷的"，然后再也不回头时，你从中学到的东西非常有限。
