# 如何像战略天才一样思考（5D思维）

# How to think like a strategic genius (5d thinking)

Your ability to think determines the outcome of your life.你思考的能力决定了你人生的结局。

That is not an exaggeration, and learning to think has never been more important.这不是夸张，学会如何思考从未像现在这样重要。

Especially if you consider yourself a smart person, because you are the most likely to think like an idiot.特别是如果你认为自己很聪明，因为你最可能像个傻瓜一样思考。

I mean, it's all over social media. There isn't a day that goes by where you see a person with a verifiably high IQ fall into obvious traps.我的意思是，社交媒体上到处都是这样的例子。每天都能看到高智商的人掉进明显的陷阱。

There are plenty of smart people who live in their mom's basement who are one french fry away from a heart attack, and there are plenty of dumb people who are abnormally happy, healthy, and wealthy.有很多聪明人住在妈妈家的地下室里，离心脏病发作只差一根薯条的距离；也有很多"笨蛋"异常地快乐、健康和富有。

Rather than talking about first-principles thinking, systems thinking, metacognition, or anything else that you can ask ChatGPT to teach you about, I want to give you something different.我不打算讨论第一性原理思维、系统思维、元认知，或任何你可以让 ChatGPT 教你的东西，我想给你一些不同的东西。

We're going to start with one-dimensional thinking and slowly work our way towards 5d thinking.我们将从一维思维开始，逐步上升到五维思维。

If you read carefully, you will experience what it's like to think on a new level.如果你仔细阅读，你将体验到在一个新层次上思考是什么感觉。

## If you're so smart, why are you broke?如果你这么聪明，为什么还破产？

> Faith is much better than belief. Belief is when someone else does the thinking. – Buckminster Fuller信念远胜于信仰。信仰是让别人替你思考。——巴克敏斯特·富勒

Thinking tip number one:第一个思维提示：

If you want to understand what something is, like genius level thinking, then it helps to understand what it is not.如果你想理解某件事物——比如天才级别的思维——那么先理解它不是什么会有所帮助。

That way, we can identify and avoid stupid thinking.这样，我们就能识别并避免愚蠢的思维。

When we observe what stupid thinking looks like, we come to a few insights:当我们观察愚蠢思维长什么样时，会得出几个洞见：

- **Stupid thinking is one-dimensional.** People try to jam everything into their own perspective and have difficulty seeing outside of it.**愚蠢思维是一维的。** 人们试图把所有东西都塞进自己的视角，难以跳出去看。
- **Stupid thinking is reductionistic.** Experts in one domain, like business, try to reduce everything to a "strategy" problem, as an example.**愚蠢思维是还原论的。** 例如，某个领域的专家（比如商业领域）试图把所有东西都还原为一个"策略"问题。
- **Stupid thinking is tribal.** You only trust your group, party, or tribe and consider everyone else wrong because they don't conform.**愚蠢思维是部落化的。** 你只信任你的群体、党派或部落，认为其他所有人都是错的，因为他们不服从你的认同。
- **Stupid thinking does not question.** Your justification is "that's just how it's done."**愚蠢思维不质疑。** 你的理由是"事情就是这样做的。"

As a whole, stupid thinking is about closing your mind off once you've reached the limits of what you know.总的来说，愚蠢思维是在你触及所知边界的那一刻就关闭了自己的思维。

Stupid thinking is to stop thinking too early. To reach a point where you react with your pre-programmed thoughts and fail to reach any novel insight.愚蠢思维就是过早停止思考。达到一个程度，你用预先编程好的想法做出反应，无法得出任何新颖的洞察。

With that, we can start to guess at a worthy definition of genius thinking, which could be:基于此，我们可以开始推测天才思维的一个有价值定义：

The ability to hold threatening ideas in the realm of possibility, paired with the intention to understand rather than just know. The mark of genius thinking is illustrated by the width, depth, and height at which you can think without being cut off from venturing further, often due to holding an idea as absolute. It's your ability to traverse the full, universal web of ideas (reality, all potential knowledge) and pull them together into something coherent or useful, if not something entirely new.将令人不安的想法保持在可能性的领域中，并带有理解的意图而非仅仅知道的意图。天才思维的标志体现在你能思考的宽度、深度和高度上，且不会因为将某个想法视为绝对真理而切断进一步探索的可能。这是一种穿越完整、普遍的 idea 之网（现实、所有潜在知识）并将其整合为连贯或有用之物的能力，即使不能创造出全新的事物。

The question then is, what's the difference between knowing and understanding? And how do you develop width, depth, and height of thought? What if you can take it even further than that?那么问题是：知道（knowing）和理解（understanding）之间的区别是什么？你如何发展思维的宽度、深度和高度？如果你能走得更远呢？

Knowing is horizontal. You learn a lot and memorize facts about a specific domain until you become competent. People who know a lot are "experts," but we don't need more experts in today's world.知道是横向的。你大量学习并记忆某个特定领域的知识，直到变得熟练。掌握很多知识的人被称为"专家"，但当今世界我们不需要更多的专家。

Understanding is vertical. It's about how sophisticated your entire cognitive operating system is. One can understand a lot while knowing very little and use that insight to take an action that leads to a more worthwhile result.理解是纵向的。这关乎你整个认知操作系统的精密程度。一个人可以在知之甚少的情况下理解很多，并利用这种洞察采取行动，获得更有价值的结果。

The "smart but dumb" phenomenon stems from being horizontally advanced but vertically stuck. A businessman who has all the money in the world but finds himself unhappy. A creative with beautiful work that can't make a living. A meathead that can't hold a steady relationship. They know a lot in their respective domain, but can't see outside of their bubble of knowledge, and that bubble of knowledge does not provide enough surface area to identify and solve problems that lead to suffering."聪明但愚蠢"现象源于横向发达但纵向停滞。一个拥有世界上所有金钱却感到不快乐的商人。一个作品优美却无法营生的创作者。一个无法维持稳定关系的笨蛋。他们在各自领域知道很多，但无法看到知识泡沫之外，而那个泡沫没有提供足够的表面积来识别和解决导致痛苦的问题。

That's why learning how to think is so important.这就是学会如何思考如此重要的原因。

Because your mind is how you interact with reality.因为你的心灵是你与现实互动的方式。

You process information → make sense of it (thinking) → make a choice → receive information as feedback → respond to that feedback and repeat the cycle.你处理信息 → 理解它（思考） → 做出选择 → 接收信息作为反馈 → 对反馈做出回应 → 重复这个循环。

Thinking, then, determines the outcome of your life.因此，思考决定了你人生的结局。

Every passing moment where you engage in stupid thinking contributes to a compounding effect, digging you into a rut so deep you can't even access the idea that would lead you out.每一个你进行愚蠢思维的当下都会产生复利效应，把你挖进一个如此深的沟壑，以至于你甚至无法接触到那个能把你带出去的想法。

## Altitudes, levels, and lines of thinking altitudes, levels, and lines of thinking altitudes, levels, and lines of thinking altitudes, levels, and lines of thinking 思维的高度、层次与线条

> You can't solve a problem from the same level of consciousness that created it. – Albert Einstein你无法在创造问题的同一个意识层次上解决问题。——阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦

Let's work from the ground up.让我们从基础开始。

**Lines** of thinking are domains you can know a lot about.**线条**（Lines）是你可以深入了解的知识领域。

Lines represent horizontal development (width).线条代表横向发展（宽度）。

These can be anything. Marketing, astrophysics, politics, social dynamics, religion, etc. When you attempt to learn domain specific knowledge, you are increasing the width of that line of thinking. It's like gaining experience in a video game.可以是任何东西。营销、天体物理、政治、社会动力学、宗教等等。当你尝试学习特定领域的知识时，你正在扩展那条思维线条的宽度。就像在电子游戏中获得经验值一样。

(By the way, if you've studied Ken Wilber at all, you can see what I'm getting at here, just applied to thinking).（顺便说一句，如果你研究过肯·威尔伯，你就能看出我在说什么——只是把它应用到思维上）。

> ![Lines of thinking](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/G_r5z4DbwAAoZOS.png)
> *图：Lines of thinking — Thinking dimensions: width (lines/domains), depth (levels of consciousness), height (altitude/average across domains)*

**Levels** of thinking are how you think about each line.**层次**（Levels）是你如何思考每一条线的方式。

Levels represent vertical development (depth).层次代表纵向发展（深度）。

When we observe how cognitive development evolves in collectives and individuals over time, we can notice 5 core patterns:当我们观察认知发展在集体和个人中如何随时间演变时，我们可以注意到5个核心模式：

- **Instinctual (Level 0)** – You are born act out of the need for pure survival. You react to stimuli with little thinking in between.**本能型（0层）** ——你出生后出于纯粹生存的需要而行动。你对刺激做出反应，中间几乎没有思考。
- **Conformist (Level 1)** – Black and white thinking. You follow rules and obey authority without questioning. You adopt others' perspective.**墨守成规型（1层）** ——非黑即白的思维。你不质疑地遵守规则、服从权威。你采纳他人的视角。
- **Individualist (Level 2)** – Critical thinking emerges and you construct your own model. You build your own perspective.**个人主义型（2层）** ——批判性思维出现，你构建自己的模型。你建立自己的视角。
- **Synthesist (Level 3)** – You see your model as one among many. You hold contradictions and use perspectives as tools rather than law or some unquestionable truth.**综合型（3层）** ——你将你的模型视为众多模型之一。你接纳矛盾，将视角作为工具而非法则或某种不容置疑的真理。
- **Generative (Level 4)** – You create original perspectives that didn't exist before, or you come to ideas without outside influence.**生成型（4层）** ——你创造出以前不存在的新视角，或者在没有外部影响的情况下得出想法。

Levels 1 and 2 can be considered "first tier" thinking, which is overly dogmatic. I'm right you're wrong.第1层和第2层可被视为"第一层级"思维，非常教条主义。我是正确的，你是错误的。

Levels 3 and 4 can be considered "second tier" thinking, which reject dogma and seek the ultimate truth that lies in the middle.第3层和第4层可被视为"第二层级"思维，它们拒绝教条，寻求存在于中间地带的终极真理。

In the domain of meaning, you are often indoctrinated into one belief system, then you question that belief system and come to your own conclusions (often rejecting the prior), then you start to see truths in all belief systems, and finally you have the ability to create a more expansive one.在意义的领域，你通常先被灌输一种信仰体系，然后质疑它并得出自己的结论（通常是拒绝前者），然后你开始在所有信仰体系中看到真理，最终你有能力创造一个更宏大的信仰体系。

In politics, much of the fighting and violence we see is due to the general population operating within levels 1 and 2. My group vs yours. Neither side can think beyond their bubble.在政治领域，我们看到的大部分争斗和暴力是由于普通大众停留在第1层和第2层。我的群体对抗你的群体。双方都无法跳出自己的泡沫思考。

Again, and I want to make this exceedingly clear because it is such an easy trap to fall into:再次强调，我想把这一点说得非常清楚，因为这太容易掉进陷阱了：

Genius thinking is the ability to continue thinking.天才思维是持续思考的能力。

At each level of thinking, your ability to navigate that space of both known and unknown ideas continues to expand. When you are born, you don't really think. When you are a conformist, you think until you reach a point where you have "the" answer, then you start defending that answer. When you are an individualist, you do the same thing, but with your answer. When you reach synthesist, you can think far and wide, but your ability to create new lines of thought is not fully developed yet.在每一个思维层次上，你在已知和未知 idea 的空间中进行导航的能力持续扩展。你出生时，你并不真正思考。当你是墨守成规者时，你思考直到达到一个点——你有了"那个"答案，然后你开始捍卫那个答案。当你是个个人主义者时，你做同样的事，但用的是你自己的答案。当你达到综合型时，你可以思考得很远很广，但你创造新思维线条的能力还未完全发展。

Reaching your highest ability to think, and thus providing the runway for your highest potential in this life, boils down to the simple practice of noticing when your mind feels threatened, being honest with yourself, and at minimum, staying open to new perspectives.达到你最高的思考能力，从而为你此生最高的潜力提供跑道，归结为一个简单的练习：注意到你的心灵何时感到受威胁，对自己有起码的诚实，并且至少保持对新视角的开放。

**Altitude** of thinking is the average of all your levels.**高度**（Altitude）是所有层次的平均水平。

Altitude represents cross-dimensional development (height).高度代表跨维度发展（高度）。

> ![Altitude of thinking](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/G_r56JSWkAAgRxI.png)
> *图：Altitude of thinking — The average level across all lines of thinking determines your overall cognitive altitude*

To best understand this, and to almost complete the thinking puzzle, it helps to visualize thinking as a skill tree.为了最好地理解这一点——并几乎完成思维拼图——把思维想象成技能树会有所帮助。

In a video game, you can put "points" into certain traits that allow you to do more inside the game. You can take on higher challenges, have more fun, and continue playing. The same holds true with the mind.在电子游戏中，你可以把"点数"分配到某些特质上，让你能在游戏中做更多的事。你可以接受更高难度的挑战，获得更多乐趣，并继续玩下去。心灵也是如此。

The thing is, higher level traits can only be unlocked once you meet a specific requirement of lower level traits. You can't access "level 3" of dexterity without reaching "level 4" of strength, as an example.问题是，更高层次的特质只有在满足低层次特质的特定要求后才能解锁。例如，如果没有达到"力量4级"，你就无法获得"灵巧3级"。

In the real world, imagine you started a business. You learned everything about marketing, sales, and product. You're verifiably smart. You make a good amount of money. But then you try to take it further, and you hit an invisible road block. You can't identify what's wrong and then blame it on what you know: "the market just isn't big enough," or some permutation of that. Your mind is closed and there is no way forward.在现实世界中，假设你创业了。你学到了关于营销、销售和产品的所有知识。你确实很聪明，赚了不少钱。但当你试图更进一步时，你撞上了一堵隐形的墙。你无法识别问题所在，然后把你知道的当作借口："市场不够大"，或类似的变体。你的心门关闭了，没有前进的路。

Further, business isn't all there is to life, obviously. If the rest of your life is falling apart, you, as a business person, will try to jam a circle into a square hole. You may relieve the issue temporarily, but you never understand the root cause.此外，商业显然不是生活的全部。如果你的生活其他方面正在崩溃，作为一个商人，你会试图把圆塞进方孔。你可能暂时缓解了问题，但你永远不了解根本原因。

This is mostly why the domains of politics, religion, and others like nutrition are so hostile and polarizing. It's a bunch of smart but dumb people calling each other idiots because they've stopped thinking and only defend what they believe is the one right way.这就是为什么政治、宗教和营养等领域如此充满敌意和极化的主要原因。这是一群聪明但愚蠢的人在互相指责对方是白痴，因为他们已经停止了思考，只捍卫他们相信的"唯一正确道路"。

Back to business, the problem is that you can't see the actual problem. It could be that you don't know how to lead a team. It could be a time management issue. It could even be a spiritual issue, but you're too business minded to even entertain that possibility. At a minimum, that perspective could birth the insight that leads to further progress. This is why generalists and polymaths win.回到商业，问题是你看不到真正的问题。可能是你不知道如何领导团队。可能是时间管理的问题。甚至可能是精神层面的问题，但你太商业化了，以至于无法接受那种可能性。至少，那种视角可能孕育出通向进一步进步的洞察。这就是为什么通才和博学者会胜出。

In other words, you can't reach Level 3 of business because you haven't "put points" into the other required domains that would unlock that level.换句话说，你无法达到商业的第3层，因为你还没有把"点数"分配到解锁那一层所需的其它领域。

## How to unlock 4-dimensional thinking 如何解锁四维思维

There is a fourth dimension to thinking.思维还有第四个维度。

Well, it's not really a fourth dimension like time, but it does increase your ability to think by the power of four.嗯，它实际上不是像时间那样的第四维度，但它确实以四倍的力量增强了你的思维能力。

You see, there are 4 "dimensions" of reality.你看，现实有4个"维度"。

These dimensions are perspectives. You can change how you look at a belief, idea, situation, or problem in 4 major ways.这些维度就是视角。你可以用4种主要方式改变你看待某个信念、想法、情境或问题的方式。

> ![4 dimensions of thinking](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/G_r6CpibAAMHR-3.png)
> *图：4 Dimensions of Thinking — A matrix of Inner/Outer × Individual/Collective perspectives*

There's the inner world and the outer world, and there's collective and individual segments of both.有内心世界和外部世界，每个世界又都有集体和个体的层面。

The **inner world** is mental. By that I mean psychological and cultural. Your individual inner psychology contains your thoughts, emotions, beliefs, and consciousness. The collective inner world, or culture, contains group beliefs, value systems, and ideologies.**内心世界**是精神性的。我指的是心理和文化。你个人的内在心理包含你的思想、情感、信念和意识。集体内心世界，即文化，包含群体信仰、价值体系和意识形态。

The **outer world** is physical, which sounds less woo-woo, but you can't deny that you can think about the mental, which is what we're here to talk about. Your individual outer world is your visible appearance, behaviors, and physical brain states or measurements. The collective outer world is composed of systems, structures, and social institutions.**外部世界**是物理性的，这听起来没那么玄乎，但你无法否认你可以思考精神层面的东西，而这正是我们要讨论的。你个人的外在世界是你可见的外表、行为和物理大脑状态或测量。集体外在世界由系统、结构和社交机构组成。

To illustrate the power of understanding this, let's walk through a thinking progression.为了说明理解这一点的重要性，让我们走一遍思维递进的例子。

- I learn a lot about marketing and use that knowledge (line of thinking)- 我大量学习营销并使用这些知识（思维线条）
- I practice, fail, and experience enough to understand that there isn't "one proven framework," but I have such an understanding that I can create outperforming marketing campaigns of my own (levels of thinking)- 我练习、失败，经历了足够多之后理解到没有"一个已被证实的框架"，但我有了足够的理解，可以创造出超越他人的营销活动（思维层次）
- I pursue interests like psychology, fitness, and personal development which gives me deeper pattern recognition that further removes me from dogmatic frameworks (altitude of thinking)- 我追求心理学、健身和个人发展等兴趣，这给了我更深入的模式识别能力，进一步让我脱离教条框架（思维高度）

From there, things start to get interesting, and it can go in so many different ways.从那里开始，事情变得有趣起来，而且可以朝着许多不同的方向发展。

One thing we forgot to mention is that thinking is usually done for the sake of solving a problem. That's what humans do. That's how we grow. That's what we find fulfillment in. And the minute you stop growing (and thus thinking), problems multiply and life becomes ever more chaotic.我们忘了一件事：思考通常是为了解决问题而进行的。这是人类在做的事。这是我们成长的方式。这是我们找到成就感的方式。而当你停止成长（也就是停止思考）的那一刻，问题会成倍增加，生活会变得越来越混乱。

So, if we identify a problem like "the world is becoming corrupt and meaningless," we can continue our thinking in that direction.所以，如果我们确定了一个问题，比如"世界正在变得腐败和没有意义"，我们可以在那个方向上继续思考。

How does society control attention and market their ideas to the masses? (collective inner world)社会如何控制注意力，向大众推销他们的想法？（集体内心世界）

How can your behavior change, more than it already has during this process, to have a positive impact on society? What would be the steps to reaching such a point of influence that you can leave your mark? (individual outer world)你的行为如何改变——比在这个过程中已经改变得更多——才能对社会产生积极影响？达到那样有影响力的程度需要什么步骤？（个人外在世界）

What does the current job market look like? Is a job an actual path to achieving such a thing? What about AI? Can I utilize the technology available to me to have that impact? (collective outer world)目前的就业市场是什么样的？工作真的是实现这一目标的途径吗？人工智能呢？我可以利用我现有的技术来产生那种影响吗？（集体外在世界）

Am I thinking about this the right way? Or is there more? What am I missing, and how does this make me feel? Motivated? Inspired? Hateful? (individual inner world)我这样思考对吗？还是说还有更多？我遗漏了什么？这让我感觉如何？受到激励？受启发？还是愤恨？（个人内心世界）

We could go on and on, but I think you get the point.我们可以没完没了地继续下去，但我想你已经明白了。

Genius thinking is the ability to direct attention in a useful direction. You gradually encourage your mind to zoom further and further out, and from there you have a more enlightened vantage point to zoom in on the original problem you sought to solve.天才思维是将注意力导向有用方向的能力。你逐步鼓励自己的心灵越拉越远地"放大"，从那里，你获得了一个更有见地的视角，来"缩小"聚焦你最初想要解决的问题。

## How to tap into the 5th dimension 如何进入第五维度

> It is no measure of health to be well‑adjusted to a profoundly sick society. – Krishnamurti适应一个病态的社会绝不是什么健康的衡量标准。——克里希那穆提

Yes, I know, this isn't actually the 5th dimension.是的，我知道，这不是真正的第五维度。

It's the fourth dimension (time), but I'm trying to practice my thinking here by making things up, and when you apply time to all four dimensions of reality we just talked about, your ability to think continues to rocket upward.它是第四维度（时间），但我在这里试着通过创造来练习我的思维——当你把时间应用到我们刚才讨论的现实四个维度上时，你的思维能力会继续飞速提升。

In all previous examples, we never tapped into the power of history. And if you understand history, specifically the evolutionary patterns rather than memorizing the events, you can aim your thinking in a more accurate direction.在所有之前的例子中，我们从未挖掘历史的力量。而如果你理解历史——特别是演化模式而非死记事件——你就能把思维导向更准确的方向。

This illustrates the difference between knowing and understanding. You can know a lot of facts about specific moments in history, and that's a great thing, but if you can't zoom out to see the essence of what that moment represents, you may fail to spot that pattern across different domains.这说明了知道和理解之间的区别。你可能知道很多历史特定时刻的事实，这很好，但如果你无法放大视角看到那个时刻所代表的本质，你可能无法在不同领域中发现那个模式。

There are 5 things you need to know to begin thinking effectively about history. These aren't exhaustive, and there is far more to it, but this is a good enough starting point.有5件事你需要知道，才能开始有效地思考历史。这些并非详尽无遗，还有更多内容，但这是足够好的起点。

**1) The master pattern is Transcend and Include**1) 主模式是"超越并包含"

A materialist would say that reality is composed of atoms, and they'd be correct.唯物主义者会说现实由原子组成，他们是对的。

A mentalist would say that reality is composed of qualia, and they'd be correct.唯心主义者会说现实由感受组成，他们也是对的。

But if we zoom out for the sake of understanding, the pattern is clear that reality is composed of whole parts (wholes that are also parts of a greater whole).但如果我们从理解的角度放大视角，模式就很清楚了：现实由整体-部分组成（这些整体同时也是更大整体的组成部分）。

Matter → Life → Mind物质 → 生命 → 心灵

Word → Sentence → Paragraph词语 → 句子 → 段落

Machine → Computer → Artificial Intelligence机器 → 计算机 → 人工智能

Human → House → City → State → Country → Continent → Planet → Solar system → Galaxy人类 → 房屋 → 城市 → 国家 → 大洲 → 星球 → 太阳系 → 星系

Each is a whole that transcends and includes the one before it. Meaning, if you remove the thing that allows another thing to exist, the entire chain self-destructs (which is why environmentalists are so worried about climate change. If the biosphere collapses, humans go down with it).每一个都是超越并包含前一个的整体。也就是说，如果你移除让另一个事物得以存在的东西，整个链条就会自我瓦解（这就是为什么环保人士如此担心气候变化。如果生物圈崩溃，人类也会随之灭亡）。

This will make sense with examples.例子会让你理解这一点。

**2) How individuals evolve physically**2) 个体如何物理性演化

This one is simple since most people can see it, touch it, smell it, etc.这一点很简单，因为大多数人都能看到、触摸到、闻到它。

Atoms → Cells → Molecules → Organs → Organism原子 → 细胞 → 分子 → 器官 → 有机体

You can think about the physical world by how individual things develop, not just humans.你可以思考物理世界是如何发展的，不仅是人类。

**3) How individuals evolve mentally**3) 个体如何心理性演化

Humans develop through an increasing circle of care.人类通过不断扩大的关怀圈发展。

First, we care about ourselves and our survival (egocentric).首先，我们关心自己和我们的生存（自我中心）。

Then, we care about our tribe, group, or culture (ethnocentric).然后，我们关心我们的部落、群体或文化（种族中心）。

Next, we care about all groups regardless of differences (worldcentric), but since the master pattern is transcend and include, we still care about ourselves and our tribe, and that must be accounted for in our thinking. We do not sacrifice ourselves or our values simply because caring about others sounds virtuous. This is usually where the arguments between most groups happen, because some use "caring about the equality of everyone" as a moral high ground that can often be self-destructive, which is counterproductive to caring about everyone.接下来，我们关心所有群体，不分差异（世界中心）——但由于主模式是"超越并包含"，我们仍然关心自己和我们的部落，这在我们的思考中必须被考虑进去。我们不会仅仅因为关心他人听起来很高尚就牺牲自己或我们的价值观。这通常是大多数群体之间发生争论的地方，因为有些人把"关心每个人的平等"作为道德高地，而这往往是自我毁灭的，与关心每个人的初衷背道而驰。

At the most developed levels, we care about reality as the ultimate whole. This clearly requires one to be able to think through vast amounts of complexity to come away with anything practical.在最发达的层次上，我们关心现实本身作为终极整体。这显然要求一个人能够处理巨大的复杂性，并从中得出任何实用的东西。

**4) How societies evolve physically**4) 社会如何物理性演化

Social structures follow technology.社会结构跟随技术演变。

We went from tribes to villages with the invention of the hoe that enabled small scale farming.随着锄头（使小规模农业成为可能）的发明，我们从部落走向村庄。

We went from villages to empires with the invention of the horse-drawn plow (because a surplus of food allowed people to focus less on farming and more on exploring, discovering and conquering.)随着马拉犁的发明（因为食物的盈余使人们能够少专注于农业，多专注于探索、发现和征服），我们从村庄走向帝国。

We went from empires to nation-states with the invention of machine agriculture.随着机械化农业的发明，我们从帝国走向民族国家。

Now, with computers in our pockets and AI at our doorstep, I'll let you think about where things could go.现在，随着口袋里的电脑和人工智能的到来，我让你自己思考接下来会走向何方。

**5) How societies evolve mentally**5) 社会如何心理性演化

Since individuals compose the collective, societies evolve through a similar pattern of egocentric, ethnocentric, and worldcentric.由于个体构成了集体，社会通过自我中心、种族中心和世界中心的类似模式演化。

Collectives have an identity too.集体也有自己的身份认同。

The tribe can only care about itself and its survival. Then, it can expand into a larger empire or nation-state, holding multiple sub-groups within it.部落只能关心自己和自己的生存。然后，它可以扩展成更大的帝国或民族国家，在其内部容纳多个子群体。

In this case, we call these worldviews pre-rational, rational, and post-rational. First we conformed to authority, then we sought progress through science (the enlightenment), then we began to realize the blind spots of both while integrating the good parts.在这种情况下，我们称这些世界观为前理性的、理性和后理性的。首先我们服从权威，然后我们通过科学寻求进步（启蒙运动），然后我们开始意识到两者的盲点，同时整合好的部分。

But how would you actually use this knowledge to "think?"但你实际上如何使用这些知识来"思考"？

Here's a few ways out of many:以下是许多方法中的几种：

Identify where individuals are when you encounter an idea, movement, or conflict. Who do you want as a politician when entering the age of AI?当你遇到一个想法、运动或冲突时，识别个体处于哪个层次。当你进入人工智能时代时，你希望什么样的人成为政治家？

When engaging in debate (political, business, personal) notice the level people are operating from. Individuals at higher levels of development often need to meet people where they are.当参与辩论（政治的、商业的、个人的）时，注意人们是从哪个层次运作的。处于更高发展层次的个体通常需要到对方所在的地方去。

Look at the technology of societies and organizations. A business still operating on industrial era methods (and are too dogmatic to adapt) will probably fail.看看社会和组织的科技。一家仍在用工业时代方法运营的企业（而且太教条主义无法适应）很可能会失败。

The most useful application is to locate where your own center of gravity is. I would encourage you to reread this letter at another time while thinking about your own development.最有用的应用是定位你自己的重心在哪里。我建议你另找时间重读这封信，同时思考你自己的发展。

## We missed something important 我们漏掉了重要的事

Stupid thinking is when you stop thinking.愚蠢思维就是停止思考。

We know that.我们知道这一点。

But what makes a person stop thinking? What makes them turn from open to defensive, closing themselves off to further learning and growth?但是什么让一个人停止思考？是什么让他们从开放变成防御，关闭自己，拒绝进一步学习和成长？

The answer is identity.答案是身份认同。

Specifically, latching onto ideas, beliefs, or constructs and making them a part of who you are.具体来说，紧紧抓住想法、信念或概念，并把它们变成你身份的一部分。

Because if you are a diehard republican or democrat, you will never be able to think outside of that bubble. Therefore, you will never be able to find the truth of the situation. That doesn't mean that you can't adopt certain values from either side, it simply means that you don't need to feel threatened when someone else disagrees with you.因为如果你是一个死忠的共和党人或民主党人，你就永远无法跳出那个泡泡思考。因此，你就永远无法找到情况的真相。这并不意味着你不能采纳任何一方的某些价值观，它只是意味着当别人不同意你时，你不需要感到受威胁。

The same applies to identifying with a specific religion, group, vocation, or anything else we've discussed prior.这同样适用于认同某个特定的宗教、群体、职业，或我们之前讨论过的任何其他东西。

You must also consider how you came to those beliefs.你还必须考虑你是如何形成那些信念的。

Most people, in today's world, have never thought for themselves a single day in their life.在当今世界，大多数人一生中从未为自己思考过一天。

They were born into a specific part of the world.他们出生在世界的某个特定地方。

That part of the world was at a specific level of development.世界的那个地方处于特定的发展层次。

They were told what to believe by parents who probably hadn't questioned their own beliefs.他们被告知要相信什么——由那些可能从未质疑过自己信念的父母告诉他们。

Those beliefs shaped how far you could think, which probably wasn't very far.这些信念塑造了你思考的范围，而那个范围可能并不远。

And remember, your mind is how you interact with reality.记住，你的心灵是你与现实互动的方式。

You process information → make sense of it (thinking) → make a choice → receive information as feedback → respond to that feedback and repeat the cycle.你处理信息 → 理解它（思考） → 做出选择 → 接收信息作为反馈 → 对反馈做出回应 → 重复这个循环。

The space in which you can think determines the outcome of your life.你能思考的空间决定了你人生的结局。

If you only think within the confines set by your parents, teachers, employer, and peers on social media, the outcome of your life may not be that pretty. Just observe where the average person ends up and it's not hard to see.如果你只在父母、老师、雇主和社交媒体上的同龄人设定的范围内思考，你人生的结果可能不会太好看。观察一下普通人最终走向哪里就不难看出这一点。

So, the next time you feel threatened, do not collapse in on yourself.所以，下次你感到受威胁时，不要向内崩溃。

Sit with it. Question it. Realize that your mind wants to feel safe, because that's how it's wired.与它共处。质疑它。意识到你的心灵想要感到安全，因为它就是那样被接线的。

Our brain is still wired like our hunter-gatherer ancestors, but we do not live in that world anymore, and the world will only change faster.我们的大脑仍然像我们的狩猎采集祖先那样接线，但我们已经不再生活在那个世界了，而世界只会变化得越来越快。

Those who practice thinking will win.那些练习思考的人会胜出。

– Dan
